With a folder chosen, files are written straight to front/ and back/ inside it. Otherwise they download as name_front.png / name_back.png.

Both halves are written at identical canvas dimensions, figure bottom-anchored — that is what makes background-size:contain scale them equally and land their feet on one line. Nothing is rescaled, so a genuine height difference between the two views survives.
