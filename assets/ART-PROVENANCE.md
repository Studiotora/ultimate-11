# Art provenance

`assets/liguria-materials-v2.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 7 October 2026. The master was retained and copied into this map. It supplies plaster, roof tiles, paving and shutters. At runtime, `art.js` samples the four quadrants into 128-pixel textures, uses nearest magnification, and maps them in world units. Plaster contrast is reduced in the shader to keep the façades readable. This is AI-generated pixel-style texture art, not a claim of hand-painted authorship.

All water effects are code, with live scene reflections: `hd2d-water.js` (Studio Tora Lab module) driven by the adapter in `water.js`. Pixel foliage, window details, wood and small stone variation are deterministic canvas artwork. The approved character sheets and Three.js remain shared Ultimate Eleven assets.

## Exact image prompt

Create a production-ready texture atlas for a detailed HD-2D pixel-art videogame set on the waterfront of Genova, Liguria, Italy. EXACT layout: square image, four equally sized square texture tiles in a strict 2 by 2 grid, no gutters, no borders, no text, no symbols. Upper left: aged warm ivory lime plaster, varied broad chipped and rubbed patches, tiny fissures, some irregular exposed ochre brick at patches; mostly plaster. Upper right: muted terracotta barrel roof tiles, closely packed curved overlapping ceramic coppi tiles, irregular warm brick reds and sienna oranges, dark crevices and chipped pale edges. Lower left: traditional weathered Ligurian grey-beige stone paving, small irregular rectangular stones in staggered courses, dark mossy joints, worn pale edges, occasional chips and green lichen. Lower right: old dark green painted wooden shutters, close horizontal slats with aged rubbed paint and subtle woodgrain, clear shadow lines. All four are flat orthographic texture swatches, NO objects, no perspective, no window frames, no cast shadow, no 3D rendered mockup. Every tile is designed to repeat seamlessly. Authentic premium handcrafted pixel art, deliberate clusters of square pixels, limited rich earth-tone palette, pixel dithering, crisp edges. NOT smooth digital painting, NOT photographic texture, NOT blurry, NOT vector blocks. Each tile should look like a carefully authored 128x128 pixel game texture enlarged with nearest-neighbor. Rich readable medium-scale wear, not random noise. Even neutral illumination baked in; the engine adds scene lighting. Output only the four-tile texture atlas.
